## Notes on tone and audience

- **X / Bluesky** — terse, leads with the most punchy fact, ends with the URL. Quote-tweet quote-skeet potential is good if it lands.
- **Facebook / NextDoor** — longer, more explanatory, ends with the "this is not anti-data-center" framing for readers who might otherwise close the tab.
- **LinkedIn** — Variant A or B (long-form), framed as professional documentation of public-record research.
- **Press / journalists** — neutral, no editorial tone, points to the verifiable record.
- **Other counties' organizing networks** — collegial, offers documentation as a service, asks for connection rather than action.

Always close with the contact email. Always link to the canonical URL. If a thread of replies turns hostile, do not engage on each one — re-link to the methodology and statement instead.

If a comment surfaces a factual error, **correct the publication** with a dated note; do not just argue in the thread. The willingness to correct is the source of trust.
