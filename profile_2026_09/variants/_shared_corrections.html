Corrections

The willingness to correct is the price of being trusted, and it is worth more when the corrections are visible than when they are quiet. Errors are fixed in place, dated, and described — not deleted. Superseded versions stay online.

The record so far, all of it public:

  • The June 2026 briefing was substantially wrong about cost, and is preserved unchanged. Its headline figures assumed an 80% Chapter 312 abatement. Parker County then adopted Resolution 26-25, declining abatements for data centers — so the briefing was describing a scenario the county had formally refused. It was rebuilt around a scenario grid. The original is still published, with its original numbers, at archive/2026-06, so anyone who read it, cited it or argued against it can see exactly what it said.
  • An entire section was retracted. A siting-risk index published in June was re-run in September and found to be defective: the score saturated for fifteen counties, and two of the published ranks could not be reproduced. The section was withdrawn and the retraction published in its place rather than the section being silently removed.
  • The site acreage was corrected from 2,075.28 to about 2,081, after a nineteenth adjacent parcel of 6.53 acres — bought from the same seller, but never issued a corrected notice — was traced in the appraisal records.
  • A rollback-tax figure was corrected from five years to three, following the 2019 amendment of Tax Code §23.55 by HB 1743.
  • A published map was upside down for three months. An argument-order bug in the plotting code mirrored the basemap vertically behind correctly drawn county boundaries. It was fixed, the chart regenerated, and the cause written into the source file.
  • A registration date was published that the record does not carry. Section 04 placed the Parker air registration in 2026, after two $500,000 campaign contributions, and interleaved contributions with project milestones so that the layout implied a sequence the filings do not establish. The TCEQ Central Registry entry carries no issuance date at all; the only date in it is the owner/operator relationship beginning March 13, 2025. The placement was withdrawn and the contributions moved to a separate section.
  • An inference about motive was printed as narration. The same section read that "Parker drew the developer's attention as the Fort Worth campus ran into resistance." No document supports it, and this publication undertakes not to infer motive from permit timing. It was removed.
  • Testimony was presented as permit fact. The TCEQ permit slide listed a 2.5-week approval timeline, an absence of public notice, continuous 24/7 operation and a "Standard Permit / Permit-by-Rule" characterization as documented features of Registration 179422. Only the regulated-entity number, registration number, status, holder and owner/operator date come from the Central Registry; the rest is testimony from the May 26, 2026 session, and the authorization mechanism was an inference that should not have been printed as a fact. Every row now carries its evidence class.
