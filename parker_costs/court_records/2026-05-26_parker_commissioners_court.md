## Hard facts established on the record

### The site
- **Address: 501 Pearson Ranch Road** (NOT "Preston" — confirmed by city manager)
- **~170 acres** is "the site" per the city manager (matches CAD parcel R000041082 at 172.78 ac); broader Black Mountain Power LLC holdings total 2,075 ac across 18 parcels per the May-15-2026 CAD corrected notices
- **Off FM 730**
- **Adjacent to City of Weatherford**, BUT
- **Previously in Weatherford ETJ; property owner petitioned to be removed** — no city regulatory control now
- **Inside Lake Weatherford watershed** (city's drinking water source)
- **~2 mi from north boundary of City of Willow Park**
- **~1 mi from a proposed $1.5B equine development** the city has been working on for 4 years
- Marcy Galley (resident) lives ~4 mi away — found out via Nextdoor 3–4 days before the meeting

### The power plant (CRITICAL — this is much smaller than I had modeled)
- **75 MW** approved by TCEQ — *not* the gigawatt scenario I was running
- **Approved for continuous operations** (24/7) — meaning the gas turbines are *primary* power, not peakers
- **Approved in 2.5 weeks** per James Hotopp, vs his own city's recent 9-month wastewater permit
- **No public process** — no notification, no public-comment period, no contested-case hearing
  - Hotopp called this "shocking" and emphasized that a wastewater permit gets months of technical review + public notice
- **5 turbines** (Sid Miller referenced "five turbines" — consistent with prior info)
- 75 MW / 5 turbines = ~15 MW per turbine = LM2500-class aero-derivatives (small frame)
- Hotopp explicit caveat: *"I don't think it would be enough for the ultimate build out for what they're talking about on this particular site"* — implying expansion is anticipated

### The owner
- **Black Mountain** is per EDC Director Strayer: *"a power company. They're oil and gas company that has land assets all over the state."*
- This confirms the Moncrief / vertical-integration hypothesis from the WA Moncrief land-consolidation pattern I found in pg01

### Hill County moratorium status (confirmed)
- Hill County passed the moratorium — **NOT unanimous**
- Hired **outside counsel**
- **State sent threatening letter** — per County Attorney, **from Senator Paul Bettencourt** specifically
- Hill County is **also facing litigation from the data center developer**
- Status: "stay tied up in court for a year or two"

### Hood County
- Different posture: had a *plat* come through, **denied based on subdivision rules + planning issue** (not a moratorium per se)
- Now in litigation

### Counties' legal authority
- **Dillon Rule applies in Texas** — counties cannot regulate what someone builds on their land
- TX Constitution 1876 — post-Reconstruction weak county government
- Counties have only statutory authority (no home-rule)
- **Ministerial duty** to approve subdivision plats meeting the rules — no discretion
- Site is **not platted** — it's raw acreage going through state permitting (TCEQ + ERCOT)

### Process timing concerns the court raised
- TCEQ permit issued in 2.5 weeks vs Weatherford wastewater 9-month process
- ERCOT load-study approvals are at state level — no county notification
- TCEQ permits filed for industrial sources don't get county notification
- Commissioner Hill: *"I think you'd be hard find to press a developer or anybody else in this room that had a TCEQ permit issued in that time frame, much less maybe a phone call returned in that amount of time."*

## What changed about the analysis

### MW corrected: 75 (initial), not 1,000
The TCEQ-approved capacity is **75 MW**, not the gigawatt scale I was modeling. My scenario has been overstating the per-family cost by roughly 13x for the *initial* facility.

The "1 GW" framing remains relevant for the **ultimate buildout** since Hotopp explicitly said the 75 MW "would not be enough." So both scenarios remain useful — 75 MW for what's permitted today, 1+ GW for what the 2,075-acre footprint suggests is planned.

### Black Mountain identity confirmed
**Oil-and-gas + power company with land assets statewide** — the Moncrief / vertical-integration hypothesis is now supported by the EDC Director's official characterization on the record.

### ETJ removal is a new significant fact
The property was actively de-annexed from Weatherford ETJ. This means:
- No city land-use control (which is why it ended up in unincorporated county)
- Deliberate maneuver — someone made this happen
- Worth checking the date and the petition record

### TCEQ permit details — 75 MW continuous
- 75 MW = 5 turbines × ~15 MW each = LM2500-class aero-derivatives (most likely)
- 24/7 continuous operations = primary power (not just backup peakers)
- 2.5-week approval = either Permit-by-Rule (size-fits, possibly), Standard Permit, or expedited NSR
- No public process = the public didn't get notice. That's the procedural irregularity.

### No abatement filed
**Black Mountain has NOT filed for Chapter 312 or 381 incentives with Parker County.** Strayer confirmed publicly on the record. The earlier project iteration was "indefinitely tabled." The political moment to set policy is **now**, before any new application.

### Bettencourt confirmed as letter source
The threatening letter to Hill County is from **State Senator Paul Bettencourt** (per County Attorney). Not the AG. Bettencourt is the chair of the Senate Committee on Local Government and has been the public face of the "counties have no authority" framing.
